Q1. What is Agentic AI in your own words?
Agentic AI is an AI system that can make decisions, use tools, and take actions by itself to complete a task.
Q2. How is Agentic AI different from a normal chatbot?
A normal chatbot only replies with text. Agentic AI can think, decide next steps, and perform actions.
Q3. One real-life example
A travel assistant that books tickets and sends reminders automatically.
